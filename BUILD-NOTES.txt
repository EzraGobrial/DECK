DECK — ONE MORE RUN

Open deck-prototype.html in a modern desktop browser. Keep the deck folder beside it.
All game assets are included locally. index.html is the entry point for static hosting.

THE OBSTACLE COLLECTION / 31 COURSES
Six packs: Open Water, High Tide, Golden Hour, After Hours, Vertical Limit, Flow State.
First Light (level one) keeps its existing track, pads, spawn and finish unchanged.
Wide Awake, Skyline and Over Under now use complete authored replacements:
linked bowls and a centre ridge; a terrace descent and landing roof; and a two-tier
crossing around an open court with its finish separated across the water.
Try the different lines and compare your runs. Route names and paint do not rank
their speed for you.
The other 23 original courses are redesigned around broad obstacle structures.
Every rebuilt course has its own authored architectural centerpiece, footprint,
sculpted riding surface and site plan. Paired craters, canyon shells, braided
ridges, amphitheatres, dunes, vaults and crescents have different shapes, not just
different placements. The remaining structures vary in dimensions and elevation;
each regular course has its own turn sequence and floor-height changes.
Four additional tower courses — Stacked, Skywell, The Spire and Tower Run — climb
through five or six floors, with a wide wraparound ascent and narrow inner ramp
between each floor. Each tower also has an optional over-vertical launch on its
first ascent. Build speed along its run-up, ride past vertical, and rotate back
to wheels-down in the air to reach the catching platform. There is no hidden
launch impulse or automatic successful flip. All courses are unlocked.

FLOW STATE adds three authored places with distinct reads. Spillway Run is a long,
broad downhill that pours into a banked catch and a speed-preserving climb-out;
the high bank is the optional carry line. Crossfade layers a wide lower ribbon
under a raised S-shaped ribbon, with a real transfer ramp to change your route.
Signal Roof is a flowing outer roof bypass or a central kicker with a real, short
gap to the elevated roof. These are structures to ride, not random pads.

The individual centerpieces are connected to supporting structures:
- Sculpted bowls with low middle lines and higher carved shoulders.
- Pitched rooftop wedges: ride the ridge or carve around either side.
- A gridded platform with real square holes in Heatwave.
- Three-way descents: wide side ramp, steep side ramp, or a fall into a middle bowl.
- Curved takeoffs into raised decks, with a longer low route around the jump.
- Slanted walls that launch across a gap into a separate landing platform.
- Round blue obstacles with inside and outside lines.
- Stepping islands with an outside bridge and two mid-route crossover ramps.
- Rolling crests, raised spines with low valleys, and three-way flyovers.

Purple-edged crossover ramps connect the bridge to the islands. Mix a few jumps
with an outside section, or leave the islands early. These are physical, rideable
connections, not teleports or forced lanes. Bowls and roofs are open across their
width, so lines can also be switched by steering across the surface.
Several courses start high, with three descending choices near the start.
Edge colors are paint for structures, not special terrain or speed rankings.

No sectors, ordered gates or checkpoints. The layout itself separates early routes
from the finish. Skilled shortcuts are allowed; falling restarts the whole run.
Cross the checker stripe to finish, even while airborne. Normal courses have no
finish height limit. Tower finishes belong to the top deck: flying above it counts,
but passing beneath it on a lower floor does not. There is no upper height limit.

HANDLING AND TOYS
Acceleration builds more gradually, now about 18% softer than the previous update.
Braking remains responsive; maximum cruising speed and boost power are unchanged.
Both jumps add a push away from the board's top side and preserve existing momentum.
Jump upright to push upward, upside down to push downward, or nose-down to push
forward. Roll sideways to direct an air jump sideways. A ground jump follows the
surface under your wheels. You still get only one extra jump before landing.
A well-aimed jump at top speed can push past the normal 43 m/s driving limit, up
to about 54 m/s. The extra-speed allowance lasts briefly and releases gradually;
it does not replace the stronger boost-pad and bumper speed allowances.
Yellow pads add a strong forward boost and a short trail matching the selected deck.
Blue jump pads remain in the preserved first course; redesigned courses use natural
ramps and jumps instead of relying on blue launch strips.
Red bumpers reflect the hit and add speed: at least 52 m/s, up to 74 m/s.
A glancing hit redirects you; a head-on hit sends you back.

There are no clean-landing bonuses and no hard-landing text notifications.
The harder you hit a surface, the more speed you lose. Impact is measured into the
surface, so following a descending ramp is gentler than slamming onto a flat deck.
Completed flips do not award bonus speed. Releasing rotation holds the airborne
angle. Land on your wheels: an upside-down or severely sideways landing stops
your momentum, displays a large red CRASH and rotates the board upright again.
The run and clock continue; there is a brief recovery before accelerating again.
Normal hard landings lose speed without a hard-landing text notification.

The camera uses stable, fixed-distance framing with no speed zoom or collision
snaps. Track geometry between the board and camera fades temporarily to keep
the view clear. Grip tape and bolts now follow the deck's exact curved profile.

CONTROLLER / STANDARD PLAYSTATION AND XBOX MAPPING
R2 / RT: accelerate
L2 / LT: brake; hold to reverse
Left stick left / right: steer
Left stick up / down in air: rotate nose down / up; hold for a full flip
Cross / A: jump, then press again for one air jump
L1 / LB: roll left in midair; hold to keep rotating
R1 / RB: roll right in midair; hold to keep rotating
R3 (right-stick click): restart the run
Options / Start: pause
Left stick / D-pad: move the outlined menu selection
Cross / A: select
Circle / B: back
Right stick on Home or in Locker: turn and tilt the board viewing angle
Play is selected automatically on the Home screen.

KEYBOARD
WASD or arrow keys: accelerate, brake, steer
Space: jump / double jump
Q / E: roll left / right in air
I / K: rotate nose down / up in air
R: restart; Esc / P: pause
Arrow keys + Enter: menus
J / L and U / O on Home or in Locker: rotate the board view

PERSONAL BESTS AND GHOST REPLAYS
The finish screen clearly shows seconds faster or slower than your personal best.
Your first finish sets the PB. A translucent, non-colliding best-run ghost appears
on later attempts. Toggle BEST GHOST in the pause menu.

Choose MY REPLAYS on Home, or WATCH MY BEST after finishing, to watch your own saved
best run from its gameplay camera. Playback does not change times, medals or tokens.
Cross / A or Start: pause / play
D-pad left / right: seek backward / forward five seconds
R3: restart playback
Circle / B: leave playback
Keyboard: Space / P, left / right arrows, R, and Esc.
Mouse: use the replay slider and visible playback buttons.
Only your own local best-run ghosts are available; online spectating is not added.

LOCKER AND SHOP
23 deck designs, including seven animated graphics; eight wheel sets, including
pulsing and polished-metal wheels. Equip decks and wheels independently.
Svens Board costs exactly 7,777 coins and has animated gold-and-black stripes with
a fixed Happy B-Day graphic on its top grip. No automatic unlock or free balance
change is applied. Each completed run earns 50 coins; new medal progress adds
its normal one-time bonus. Replay viewing, restarting and menu actions never
award coins. Cosmetics do not change physics.
There are no real-money purchases. Original, game-generated music plays in menus
and the Locker. SOUND ON/OFF is at the top right.
Press a button on a connected controller so the browser can detect it.
Some browsers require a click or keyboard press before allowing audio to start.

SAVES
The birthday update preserves coins, cosmetics and unaffected course records.
Wide Awake, Skyline and Over Under receive new layout revisions, archiving their
old course results and starting new matching best-run ghosts. The old September 6
one-time score-reset migration remains for saves that never received that earlier
update; it is not triggered again for saves already migrated. Normal reloads do
not reset progress.
Progress is local to this browser and site. Clearing browser data can remove it;
moving from the local file to a hosted URL does not carry progress to the new site.
No account, online leaderboard, payments or external service is used.

HOSTING
The outputs folder is ready for static hosting: index.html, deck-prototype.html and
the complete deck folder must stay together. A GitHub repository and its Pages
settings are still needed to publish a public playable link. This build has not
been uploaded or published automatically.

TESTING
Automated CPU checks cover first-level geometry preservation, main-route completion,
alternate routes, crossover ramps, solid sides, ceilings, high-speed landings, open
holes, airborne finishes, boosts, bumpers, impact speed loss, upside-down launches,
crash recovery, exact grip alignment, unique landmarks and one-time score clearing.
Directional-jump checks cover inverted, sideways and compound rotations, matching
the rendered board, preserving momentum, temporary overspeed and slower acceleration.
Headless DOM checks cover all scene construction, menus, simulated controller input,
PB comparisons, cosmetics, board viewing and replay controls without changing saves.
These are not a GPU browser playtest or a physical-controller playtest. Device-specific
graphics, audio and control feel still need a real play session.

This is a browser game, not a packaged PlayStation or Xbox console release.
